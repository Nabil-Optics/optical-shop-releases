🚀 How Setup & Multi-PC Sync Work

  1. First Computer (New Shop): Open the app, click Sign in with Shop Gmail (required first), then set your Shop Passphrase and Counter name (e.g. Counter 1).
  2. Second / Additional PCs: Click Join an existing shop and sign in with the same Shop Gmail (or enter the Shop Passphrase + Connect Code). All items, customers, and invoices sync automatically!
  3. Automatic Updates: Whenever a new version is published to the server, installed PCs automatically download it in the background and show "Update ready" in the top bar.
